📊 Sales & Financial Performance Power BI Dashboard
A comprehensive multi-page Power BI dashboard for analyzing sales revenue, profitability, and financial performance of a food & beverage business.
📌 Pages
1. Revenue & Margin Analysis
Top-level KPI cards for Revenue ($9.68M), Cross Margin ($4.02M), and Gross Margin % (42%) with monthly sparklines. Drill-down tables by category, product, team, and salesperson — with smart tooltips showing month-over-month variance and top performers.
2. Income Statement
Full P&L view with Revenue, Cost, Expenses, Net Income, and Net Income % (22%). Waterfall chart tracking monthly net income build-up, a detailed monthly breakdown table, and a combo chart comparing Net Income, Expenses, and Gross Margin % trends.
3. What-If Scenario Planner
Interactive scenario modeling tool with sliders for Quantity, Unit Price, Unit Cost, and Expenses. Instantly calculates variance impact on Revenue, Cost, Expenses, and Net Income — visualized across the Top 10 product groups.
✨ Features

Dynamic slicers by Month, Team, and Category
Smart KPI cards with best-month highlights and mini trend lines
Waterfall chart for cumulative income tracking
Interactive What-If parameter sliders using DAX
Drill-through tooltip popups with contextual margin insights
Consistent dark navy + pink/teal color theme across all pages

🛠️ Tools & Tech

Power BI Desktop
DAX (calculated measures, What-If parameters, time intelligence)
Data modeling across sales, product, and team tables

📁 Dataset
Food & beverage retail dataset including transactions by product group (Wheat Flours, Oils, Yeast, Coffee, Drinks, etc.), sales team assignments, and monthly financials
